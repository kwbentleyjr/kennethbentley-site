Bio

Kenneth Bentley Jr. is a New York City freelance musician specializing in tuba, sousaphone, and electric bass. Known professionally as Kenny, he performs across a wide range of musical settings, from orchestral concerts and theater productions to brass bands, touring ensembles, and contemporary crossover projects.

Kenny performs regularly with the Radio City Music Hall Orchestra and has appeared with groups including Hazmat Modine, Red Baraat, Brass Against, Sugartone Brass Band, and Slavic Soul Party. His work as a freelance musician has taken him to venues ranging from concert halls and theaters to clubs, festivals, and street parades throughout the United States and abroad.

Equally comfortable in orchestral, traditional, and contemporary settings, Kenny’s musical approach reflects a broad interest in rhythm, groove, ensemble playing, and stylistic versatility. Whether performing orchestral repertoire, second line-inspired brass band music, global fusion, or electric bass in amplified settings, he values adaptability, strong musical communication, and bringing energy to every performance.

In addition to performing, Kenny maintains an active interest in arranging, technology, and creative projects that connect music with digital media and modern tools. He continues to work throughout New York City and beyond as a performer, collaborator, and touring musician.
