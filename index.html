Kenny Bentley, Musician

I'm excited you found me! My name is Kenny Bentley and I am a freelance musician in New York City. In addition to freelance work, I play regularly with the Radio City Music Hall Orchestra, Sugartone Brass Band, Slavic Soul Party, Red Baraat, Brass Against, and Hazmat Modine.

Please click on my Bio page to find out more about me. In addition to my bio you may use this site to keep track of my performances and contact me about future performances. Also, feel free to browse my gallery to see pictures and video from my latest shows.

Thank you for visiting and I hope you enjoy kennethbentley.com!
