Welcome

I’m excited you found me! My name is Kenny Bentley, and I’m a freelance musician in New York City.

I play tuba, sousaphone, and electric bass, and I perform regularly with the Radio City Music Hall Orchestra, Sugartone Brass Band, Slavic Soul Party, Red Baraat, Brass Against, and Hazmat Modine.

Please click on my Bio page to find out more about me. You can also use this site to keep track of my upcoming performances, browse photos and videos from recent shows, or contact me about future performances.

Thank you for visiting, and I hope you enjoy the music, photos, and upcoming performances.
