Welcome

Professional tuba, sousaphone, and electric bass player based in New York City.

For more than thirty years, I have performed throughout the United States and internationally with brass bands, jazz ensembles, orchestras, theater productions, and touring artists.

My work has included performances with Hazmat Modine, Sugartone Brass Band, Slavic Soul Party, Red Baraat, Brass Against, and the Radio City Music Hall Orchestra.

This site includes my schedule, biography, photos, and contact information for bookings, recording sessions, and other professional inquiries.
